  3. BBoE: Leveraging a Bundle of Edges for Kinodynamic Bidirectional Motion Planning

    S. Bangalore Raghu and A. Roncone

    IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) · 2026 · Accepted

    Introduces BBoE, a bidirectional kinodynamic planner that leverages a bundle of edges, the only planner in the benchmark to reach 96% success on all four systems (3D to 6D) with the lowest planning time on three.

    PDF Project

  4. Efficient Multi-Robot Motion Planning with Precomputed Translation-Invariant Edge Bundles

    H. Gupta, P. Motter, A. Chakrabarty, R. Sodani, S. Bangalore Raghu, A. Roncone, B. Hayes, and Z. Sunberg

    arXiv · 2026 · Preprint

    Introduces KiTE-Extend, a planner-agnostic action selection mechanism that guides sampling-based kinodynamic planners with a library of trajectory segments computed offline, with its clearest gains in multi-robot planning.

    PDF arXiv
